# No-solder build

Constraint since 2026-10-13: the owner cannot solder, so every electrical connection must plug in and
every mechanical one must use a screwdriver. Consequences: [[seeed-ee05]] instead of the
[[custom-pcb-r1]]; panel ribbon straight into the EE05; battery on a JST plug; heat-set inserts (need a
soldering iron) replaced by self-tapping screws in the next [[frame]] version.
