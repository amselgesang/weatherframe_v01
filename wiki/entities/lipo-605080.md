# LiPo 605080 battery

1S 3.7 V, 6 × 50 × 80 mm, about 3000 mAh, with protection PCB and JST-PH 2.0 plug. Sits flat in a
fenced pocket behind the upper left of the [[frame]]. About two months per charge at a 15-minute
interval with quiet hours ([[wake-cycle]]); expected replacement after roughly 4–6 years of ageing.

Replaceable without tools beyond a screwdriver: open the back plate, release the panel ribbon, unplug.
Check polarity against the board's socket marking before the first plug-in — JST battery leads are not
standardised. A battery hatch was offered as an option (not built).
