# Seeed_GFX board pin definitions

`User_Setups/EPaper_Board_Pins_Setups.h` from Seeed_GFX (MIT): SPI, CS, DC, BUSY, RST and
`TFT_ENABLE` (panel power, GPIO43, driven HIGH) for the EE02–EE05 and EN04/EN05 boards. Authoritative
source for the [[seeed-ee05]] panel pins in the [[esphome-firmware]].
