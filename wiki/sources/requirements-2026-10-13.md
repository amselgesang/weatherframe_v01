# Owner requirements (2026-10-13)

Weather station on the owner's [[waveshare-7in5-v2-panel]], used as a wall picture frame, Wi-Fi,
rechargeable battery; frame and PCB to be designed, in Fusion 360 and KiCad where possible; made by
[[jlcpcb]]. Later: white frame, [[flip-out-stand]] at 25° with a 3 mm wall gap, engraved button labels,
wider hinge and stand, and no soldering ([[no-solder-build]]).
